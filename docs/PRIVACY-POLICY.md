# Privacy Policy — Ran

Last updated: 2026. Developer: Nawzad Rasul Mohammed, Ranya, Sulaymaniyah, Kurdistan Region, Iraq.
Contact: nawzadrasul92@gmail.com

## Short version
Ran collects nothing. There is no server, no account and no internet connection involved.
Everything you write, send, say or record stays on the phones taking part in the conversation.

## What stays on your phone
- Your name, phone number and optional profile picture
- Your contacts inside Ran, your messages, photos, videos and files, your call history
- Your security keys, created on the phone and never sent anywhere except to the people you talk to

## What leaves your phone, and to whom
Only to the other phone on the same Wi-Fi network, and only end-to-end encrypted:
- The content of your calls, messages and files
- Your name, phone number and profile picture, when you contact someone or they contact you
- If you switch on "Show me as available", your name and number are announced on that local network

Nothing is sent to the developer, to the university, or to any company. There are no adverts, no analytics
and no third-party services inside the app.

## Permissions and why
- **Microphone** — voice and video calls, voice rooms
- **Camera** — video calls and taking a profile picture
- **Notifications** — showing incoming calls and messages
- **Photos and videos** — sending media and saving received media to your gallery
- **Location (optional)** — only if you turn on "Include my location" for a safety check-in; the position is sent
  to your team members as a message and is never stored elsewhere
- **Network / Wi-Fi state** — to find phones on the same Wi-Fi and warn you when the network blocks them
- **Run in background / ignore battery optimisation** — so calls can ring while the phone is locked

## Children
Ran is a communication tool for organisations. It shows no public content and has no feed.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling Ran deletes everything it holds. Inside the app you can delete individual chats, your call
history and your profile picture at any time.

## Changes
Any change to this policy will be published with the app update that introduces it.
