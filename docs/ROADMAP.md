# Ran — what is next

## 1.0 (this release)
Local-Wi-Fi calls, video, chat, media, voice rooms, safety check-in/SOS/roll call, phone-number identity,
profile pictures, connection check, pause/resume, About, Play Store package.

## 1.1 — Groups & teams (next)
- Group chats: create a group, add members by QR or from contacts, admin can rename/remove
- One-tap "add whole team" and department groups
- Announcements: one message to a whole group, delivered when each member appears on the Wi-Fi

## 1.2 — Stories (24 hours)
- Photo/video/text stories stored on your own phone, fetched by your contacts while you are on the same Wi-Fi
- Viewer list, automatic expiry after 24 hours, "close friends" option

## 1.3 — Ran Social (local social platform)
- Posts, photos, videos and reels · likes and comments · follow people in your organisation
- Everything spreads phone-to-phone on the local network — no servers, no internet
- Reporting and blocking tools, and a moderator role for the organisation

## Ideas for later
- Department and class channels · verified staff badges · lost & found board
- Offline map of team check-ins · needs board for emergencies · export roll-call reports

## Known limits (honest list)
- Works only between devices on the **same** local network; some networks block device-to-device traffic
- iPhone is not supported: Apple does not allow an app to listen for calls in the background without internet
- Phone numbers are not verified by SMS (there is no server), so verify important contacts by QR face to face
